Medicare Coverage Options in Spring, TX

Medicare Advantage Plans (Part C)

Medicare Advantage plans are popular in Spring because they often combine hospital, medical, and prescription drug coverage into one convenient plan. Many Spring residents enjoy benefits like:

  • $0 premium options with comprehensive coverage
  • Dental cleanings, exams, and even dentures
  • Vision coverage including eyeglasses
  • Hearing aids and hearing exams
  • SilverSneakers gym membership at local fitness centers
  • Over-the-counter allowances for health items
  • Transportation to doctor appointments

Medicare Supplement Insurance (Medigap)

If you prefer the flexibility of Original Medicare, a Medigap policy can help cover your out-of-pocket costs. Supplement plans in Texas can help pay for:

  • Part A hospital deductible ($1,632 in 2024)
  • Part B coinsurance (typically 20% of approved costs)
  • Skilled nursing facility coinsurance
  • Foreign travel emergency coverage

Important Medicare Enrollment Periods

Missing an enrollment deadline can result in penalties or gaps in coverage. Here are the key dates Spring residents should know:

  • Initial Enrollment Period: 7-month window around your 65th birthday (3 months before, your birthday month, and 3 months after)
  • Annual Enrollment Period: October 15 through December 7 - review and change plans for the upcoming year
  • Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment: January 1 through March 31 - switch Medicare Advantage plans or return to Original Medicare
  • Special Enrollment Periods: Triggered by qualifying events like moving, losing employer coverage, or other life changes

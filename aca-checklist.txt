ACA / Obamacare Enrollment Checklist

1) Household information
- Household size
- Dependents
- Address and ZIP code

2) Income documents
- Recent pay stubs
- Last tax return
- Other income records

3) Current coverage details
- Existing policy information
- Employer coverage status

4) Preferred providers
- Doctors and clinics
- Prescriptions and pharmacies

5) Enrollment timing
- Qualifying life event details if outside Open Enrollment
- Preferred effective date

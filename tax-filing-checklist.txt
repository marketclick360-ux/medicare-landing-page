Tax Filing Checklist

1) Identity documents
- Social Security numbers or ITINs
- Photo ID

2) Income documents
- W-2 forms
- 1099 forms
- Self-employment income summary

3) Deduction and credit records
- Mortgage interest (1098)
- Student loan interest
- Child care expenses
- Education expenses

4) Banking details
- Routing and account number for direct deposit

5) Previous return
- Last year's tax return copy

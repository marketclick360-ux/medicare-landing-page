Life Insurance Planning Checklist

1) Coverage goal
- Income replacement years
- Debt payoff amount
- Education funding target

2) Beneficiary planning
- Primary beneficiary
- Contingent beneficiary

3) Budget range
- Preferred monthly premium range

4) Policy preferences
- Term length (10/20/30 years)
- Interest in permanent coverage

5) Health and lifestyle notes
- Tobacco status
- Major health history

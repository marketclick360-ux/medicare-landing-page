About Medicare Help Center

Who We Are

Medicare Help Center is a licensed insurance agency dedicated to helping seniors and Medicare-eligible individuals find the right Medicare coverage for their needs. We work with multiple insurance carriers to provide you with options tailored to your healthcare requirements and budget.

Our Mission: To simplify Medicare by providing free, no-obligation guidance from licensed professionals who put your needs first.

Our Services

We specialize in:

  • Medicare Advantage Plans (Part C) - Comprehensive coverage that often includes additional benefits like dental, vision, and prescription drugs
  • Medicare Supplement Plans (Medigap) - Coverage that helps pay for costs Original Medicare doesn't cover
  • Prescription Drug Plans (Part D) - Standalone coverage for your medications

Licensing Information

Medicare Help Center operates as a licensed insurance agency. Our agents are licensed to sell Medicare products in multiple states and maintain all required certifications for Medicare plan sales.

State Licenses: Our agency and agents are properly licensed in the states where we conduct business, including Texas and surrounding states. All agents complete annual AHIP (America's Health Insurance Plans) certification and carrier-specific training as required by CMS (Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services).

Our Commitment to You

  • Free Service: Our Medicare consultation services are provided at no cost to you
  • No Pressure: We provide information and guidance without high-pressure sales tactics
  • Unbiased Advice: We work with multiple carriers to find the best fit for your situation
  • Ongoing Support: We're here to help not just during enrollment, but year-round

Important Disclosures

Medicare Help Center is not connected with or endorsed by the United States government or the federal Medicare program. We are an independent licensed insurance agency.

The Medicare plans we represent are offered by private insurance companies that contract with Medicare. Enrollment in these plans depends on contract renewal.
